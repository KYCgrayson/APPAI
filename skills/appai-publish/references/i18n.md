# Languages and child pages

Read [Multi-language & multi-page](https://appai.info/spec/i18n) for locale variants, URL rules, reserved slugs and navigation. Follow its guidance to proactively create relevant locale versions and publish both `zh-TW` and `zh-CN` when publishing Chinese.

Rewrite headlines idiomatically for each audience while keeping product facts and promises consistent. Choose languages based on the audience, usually 2–3 locales: the user's language, English and one regional language. Check translated links and child-page destinations as part of the published-page review.
