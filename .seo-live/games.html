Games to play together

Small original games made at AppAI: three logic puzzles to solve on your own, a number duel for a parent and child, and a party game for the whole family around one phone.

Everything runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install, and no sign-in needed to play.

Tabletop Puzzles →

  • River Crossing

    Plan boat trips and bring every traveler safely across the river.

    Players
    1
    Ages
    6+

    How to play

    1. Choose a difficulty and read its boat capacity and riverbank rules.
    2. Select the travelers to board, then tap the boat or Cross. Bring the boat back when needed.
    3. Get every traveler to the opposite bank without breaking the rules. Undo or restart to try fewer crossings.
    Details / Play: River Crossing

  • Night Bridge

    Share one lantern and cross the bridge before the fire catches up.

    Players
    1
    Ages
    6+

    How to play

    1. Choose a difficulty and check each traveler's crossing time and the lantern's limit.
    2. Select travelers on the lantern's bank, then tap the lantern or Walk to cross. A pair takes the slower traveler's time.
    3. Send someone back with the lantern when needed. Get everyone to safety before the fire reaches them or the lantern runs out; Undo lets you revise the plan.
    Details / Play: Night Bridge

  • Water Pouring

    Fill, empty and pour between jugs to measure the target amount.

    Players
    1
    Ages
    6+

    How to play

    1. Choose a difficulty, then check the jug capacities and the target amount.
    2. Select a jug to fill or empty it. To pour, select the source and then the destination jug.
    3. Measure the target amount using the available actions. Undo or restart to look for a solution in fewer steps.
    Details / Play: Water Pouring

  • Guess Duel

    Beta

    Hide a secret number, take turns guessing, and deduce your opponent's code from the A/B clues.

    Players
    2 (or 1 vs computer)
    Ages
    8+ (3 digits for kids, 4 for a challenge)

    How to play

    1. Pick 3 or 4 different digits as your secret.
    2. Take turns guessing. A = right digit, right place. B = right digit, wrong place.
    3. Play on one device, against the computer, or pass a link between two phones.
    Details / Play: Guess Duel

  • Werewolf

    Beta

    One phone, with the app or a person as host

    Players
    3–16 players (3–12 with the app as host)
    Ages
    8+ (6–7 with family wording and a grown-up)

    How to play

    1. Pick a host: pass the phone with the app as host, or let one adult read the script.
    2. At night the werewolves choose someone, the seer checks a player, and the witch can save or poison.
    3. By day everyone talks and votes to find the werewolves.
    Details / Play: Werewolf

Questions parents ask

Which games can we play at a restaurant while waiting for food?

Try a river crossing, bridge and torch, or water jug puzzle together on one phone. Guess Duel lets two people take turns. Stop when the meal arrives; Werewolf is better when your group has time and space to talk.

Are these free online games for kids?

The games are free in your browser with no download. Check each game's age guidance and rules with a grown-up. Sign-in is needed to upload a leaderboard score, not to play.

Which are quiet waiting room games?

The three tabletop puzzles work well for quiet discussion with sound and music off. Werewolf needs group discussion and is better suited to a table away from a quiet waiting room.

Can we play while waiting in line?

Try a puzzle while the queue is stationary. Put the phone away when the line moves, and agree on a stopping point together.
